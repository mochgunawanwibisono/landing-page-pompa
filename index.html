Why Partner with Us?

Our technical catalog is built to perform in environments where standard equipment fails. Whether your operations demand extreme high-volume circulation up to 55,000 m³/hr, high-pressure head delivery reaching 850 meters, or severe thermal management spanning -50°C to 350°C, our pumping systems are engineered to withstand the harshest pressures and continuous duty cycles.

Industrial processing requires specialized handling for challenging fluids. From non-clogging solid handling units capable of passing 105 mm particles to positive displacement pumps processing high-viscosity media up to 1,000,000 cP, we ensure smooth flow across pulp, sugar, and chemical production lines. Furthermore, our API 675 compliant double-diaphragm and plunger metering pumps guarantee zero-leakage, high-precision chemical dosing for critical water treatment and process automation. We complement our pump offerings with an integrated range of industrial hardware, including control valves, electromagnetic flowmeters, agitators, and digital monitoring systems.

Based in Surabaya, our technical support team provides direct consultation, dynamic system sizing, and fast response times to keep your operations running seamlessly.
